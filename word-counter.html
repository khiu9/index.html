About Word Counter

This free word counter tool helps you analyze text content. It counts words, characters (with and without spaces), sentences, paragraphs, and lines. It also estimates reading and speaking times and extracts the most frequent keywords by filtering out common stop words. Perfect for writers, students, SEO professionals, and content creators who need quick text statistics.
